02 — Approach

More about my practice →
01 / Understand

Find the real task.

Start with the reader's goal. Establish context, prerequisites, constraints, and what success looks like.

02 / Structure

Make the path clear.

Build focused topics with explicit actions and expected results. Keep answers understandable when retrieved on their own.

03 / Verify

Trust the evidence.

Check against product behavior, technical sources, and engineering input. Use automation and AI without handing over editorial judgment.
