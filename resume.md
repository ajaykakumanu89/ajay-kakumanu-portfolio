## AI-assisted documentation approach

- Use AI tools for research, early drafts and editorial checks; evaluate outputs against product behavior, source code, tickets and SME feedback before publication.
- Write documentation intended for both people and AI-assisted discovery through concise task-based topics, precise terminology, source-grounded content and repeatable quality checks.
- Optimize topics for a knowledge assistant so the right passage is retrieved: one question per topic, the answer in the first sentence, the product version named, and foundational procedures kept separate from expert troubleshooting.
- Check a topic the way the assistant will read it, as a single retrieved chunk, and rewrite it when the answer depends on another section or could mix two workflows.
